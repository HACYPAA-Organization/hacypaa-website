About HACYPAA

Heartland Area

Recovery was never
meant to happen alone.

HACYPAA is the Heartland Area Conference of Young People in Alcoholics Anonymous. It brings young people and the young at heart together for a weekend centered on recovery, unity, service, fellowship, and fun.

You do not need to have everything figured out before attending. You only need a willingness to show up. HACYPAA creates space for people to connect, share experience, and discover that none of us must walk through recovery alone.

  • 01 Recovery

    Honest stories, shared experiences, and practical hope for a new way forward.

  • 02 Unity

    Different people and different paths brought together through one common purpose.

  • 03 Service

    Showing up for one another and giving away what was freely given to us.

The 2027 theme

Strength through
vulnerability

Divine Paradox explores the contradictions found throughout recovery. We surrender to win. We give away what we want to keep. We discover strength by admitting weakness and freedom by accepting help.

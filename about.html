About HACYPAA

Heartland of America

Recovery was never
meant to happen alone.

HACYPAA is the Heartland of America Conference of Young People in Alcoholics Anonymous. It brings you people and the young at heart together for a weekend centered on recovery, unity, service, fellowship, and fun.

You do not need to have everything figured out before attending. You only need a willingness to show up. HACYPAA creates space for people to connect, share experience, and discover that none of us must walk through recovery alone.

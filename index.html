About the conference

Where opposites meet

We surrender to win.
We give it away to keep it.

HACYPAA brings young people in recovery together for a weekend of connection, service, laughter, and shared experience. You do not need to have it all figured out. You only need to show up.

Divine Paradox is a celebration of the contradictions at the heart of recovery: strength through vulnerability, freedom through surrender, and belonging without having to become someone else.
